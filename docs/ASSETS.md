# Proof asset provenance

No image generation or remote asset dependency is used in the proof canvas.

- Character artwork: Open Peeps by Pablo Stanley, through `react-peeps@0.1.10` by Emre Çakır. The package declares MIT. Its complete license is copied to `public/licenses/react-peeps-MIT.txt`. Earlier research records Open Peeps' CC0 availability; provenance remains recorded here. Source: https://www.openpeeps.com/ and https://github.com/CeamKrier/react-peeps .
- `scripts/build-peeps.tsx` builds 410 local SVG variants: ten story-specific archetypes plus two legacy characters × five expressions × six poses, plus fifty archetype assets for recasting missing-pose historical records. Hair/accessories/facial hair stay distinct per archetype; bodies use actual Open Peeps ShirtBW, PointingFingerBW, RoboDanceBW, EasingBW, CrossedArmsBW and ClosedLegBW (sitting) parts. Rebuild with `pnpm art:build`. These are bundled source-derived static assets, not AI imagery. The ten original Ari/Sam assets remain untouched for compatibility. `shared/casting.ts` is the parts and name/trait index.
- UI, display and illustrated product UI: **Bricolage Grotesque**, locally bundled via `@fontsource/bricolage-grotesque@5.3.0` (https://fontsource.org/fonts/bricolage-grotesque), weights 400/500/700/800. SIL Open Font License copied verbatim to `public/licenses/Bricolage-Grotesque-OFL.txt`. Export embeds the same local 500/800 WOFF2 files. DM Sans and Bangers imports and dependencies were removed for the design probe; their historical license files remain for provenance.
- `scripts/build-comic-metrics.mjs` extracts deterministic advance widths from the bundled Comic Neue Bold WOFF into `shared/comic-metrics.ts` for server-side fit validation. It adds no dependency or font licence and does not generate imagery.
- Balloon lettering only: **Comic Neue Bold**, designed by Craig Rozynski, locally installed as `@fontsource/comic-neue@5.3.0` (https://fontsource.org/fonts/comic-neue). SIL Open Font License 1.1 copied verbatim from the package to `public/licenses/Comic-Neue-OFL.txt`. The browser text measurement loads this same 700-weight font; the export embeds the same local WOFF2. No remote font requests.
- Props are original simple SVG shapes in `shared/render-strip.ts`.
- Prepared stories in `shared/examples.ts` and the Slack-note angles/comic in `shared/v2-fixture.ts` are hand-authored, explicitly illustrative fixtures. The latter uses the supplied famous note to demonstrate fictional reactions and UI staging; it is not evidence of model generation.
- Existing user-staged `assets/icon.svg` is untouched and is not claimed as an approved Strip logo.

The renderer accepts structured scene data, not arbitrary model-supplied markup or asset URLs. Local SVG/font assets are embedded in the export SVG before PNG encoding. Art renders in finished ink. Selection, in-flight working marks, blue agent-review tags/outlines/corners and red MINE stamps are editor-only; PNG export suppresses all review/protection chrome.
